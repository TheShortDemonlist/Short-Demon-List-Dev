List Information

Welcome to The Short Demonlist, tracking the hardest ultra-short levels in Geometry Dash.

Guidelines & Requirements

  • Length Limit: Levels must be classified as Tiny or Short (under 30 seconds).
  • Video Proof: Clicks/taps must be clearly audible throughout the entire video.
  • Unedited Raw Footages: Raw audio and uncut video recording are required upon submission.
  • No Modifications: Hacks altering physics, hitboxes, or speed are strictly banned. FPS bypass is allowed up to 360 FPS.

Scoring System

Points are allocated using an exponential decay curve based on placement:

Points = 250 × (0.95)(Rank - 1)
