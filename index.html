TSDL Rules & Guidelines

Welcome to TSDL (The Short Demonlist)!

Submission Requirements

  • Level Length: Must be 20 seconds to 40 seconds long. Anything shorter or longer is not eligible for placement.
  • Decoration: Must have at least a basic, smooth amount of decoration (doesn't need to be feature-worthy, but needs some effort).
  • Public Server & ID: Must have an OFFICIAL Level ID and showcase from the main GD servers. Levels built on private servers are not allowed.
  • Star Rating: Levels do NOT need to be in-game rated as long as they are submitted with legitimate verification proof.
  • Megacolls & Sequels: Mega collaborations with more than 6 creators are not allowed. Sequels are allowed but will take extra time to review for fairness.

Verification & Client Rules

  • Geode & Noclip: Geode is fully allowed. If noclip is used, accuracy is checked deeply—if accuracy drops below 100% (e.g., 99.99%), the run is invalid.
  • CBF & CAF: Allowed only if playing above 120 FPS.
  • Platform & Level Version: Both PC and Mobile are allowed, but you MUST play on the public version of the level (no refined/edited versions).
  • Live Footage: All completions require raw, live footage of your verification/completion.

Conduct Policy

Any drama that risks getting the Demonlist taken down will result in a permanent account and stats ban. If proven innocent, your stats and submitted levels will be fully restored.

Scoring System

Points are calculated using our tier-based formula:

Top 1: 500pt | 2–5: 450–400pt | 6–10: 390–275pt | 11–30: 250–150pt
